PROBLEM STATEMENT:

Managing multiple sellers on a single shopping platform is challenging because each seller has different products, prices, inventory, and order details. Handling all this information manually can lead to errors, delays, and poor management.
          
This project proposes a Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Marketplace (Amazon-style) where multiple sellers can register, list products, manage inventory, and process orders through a single platform. Customers can easily browse, compare, and purchase products, while administrators can efficiently manage sellers, products, and transactions.
